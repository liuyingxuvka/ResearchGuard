# Citation-Grounded Reasoning and Writing

Build a claim-to-source matrix before substantive source-backed final prose. Each important claim records source ids, source role, claim strength, limitation/opposition, what the source can and cannot support, semantic-fit risk, citation marker, and exact artifact locator.

Use compact markers only when they resolve to registered sources. Separate event fact, official claim, independent report, limiting evidence, expert analysis, historical background, and hypothesis. Marker presence is not semantic fit: check wording, scope, tense, causality, execution/outcome layer, source independence, date/coverage period, and forecast-versus-observation status.

Create paragraph/page/slide blueprints with unit job, claim, support, limiting sentence when relevant, source roles/markers, allowed wording, and final treatment. Keep reader-facing prose natural to the requested genre; internal diagnostic labels and fixed audit headings do not belong in final copy.

Reconcile the source registry, source portfolio/key-number ledger when available, claim-to-source matrix, inline markers, structural contribution graph, and conclusion tournament after the last material edit. Missing or stale support routes to SourceGuard, claim downgrade, appendix/background/hypothesis treatment, omission, or human review.
