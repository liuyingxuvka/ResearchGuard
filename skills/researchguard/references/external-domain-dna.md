# External-domain DNA

Load this reference only when ResearchGuard must build, inspect, deepen, export, impact-check, or reverse-trace a portable model of one external paper, model description, test workflow, or other research object. This is member-domain DNA joined by composition transport. It is not the ResearchGuard repository software-DNA root, which remains FlowGuard-owned.

## What qualifies

ResearchGuard owns the external target boundary, declared model scope, parent `Input x State -> Set(Output x State)` mapping, hierarchy, handoffs, compact projection, impact traversal, and reverse traversal. It owns no fifth semantic evaluator. SourceGuard alone derives source roles, coverage, anchors, and material variance. TraceGuard alone derives evidence-bound order and storylines. LogicGuard alone licenses bounded conclusions and scope limits. ExperimentGuard alone derives finite comparisons and unresolved hypotheses.

Before any external-object projection, require one exact current four-member portable composition. Qualification must reopen every full `MemberModelEnvelope`, expected-target authority, native owner attestation, `MemberBehaviorManifest`, native receipt, opaque native model, and the closed member-owned proof material: LogicGuard's purpose contract/candidate/good-bad cases, SourceGuard's contract/observations, TraceGuard's task contract/good-bad models, and ExperimentGuard's frozen spec. It then executes the manifest's good transition through the member-owned replay. That replay reaches the member's `_replay_blueprint` and current `check_blueprint` without reopening the generating repository, target tree, user profile, or compiler temporary directory. Recording an entry-point string, copying an output, or replacing this with another generic evaluator is not evidence.

External admission fixes which target and denominator are in scope; member-native parsing separately determines what those admitted bytes mean. Anchor and immutable receipt locators/hashes are persistent and are replayed without treating process memory or a cache as authority. Direct member use without external admission is unverified, and a different target requires a separately admitted anchor. Production exposes no public or generic target, attestation, or receipt issuer and no inline candidate-authoring shortcut. Impact and reverse trace admit exactly one current closed composition replay over resolved evidence before returning ordinary output.

Build with the explicit composition:

```powershell
researchguard domain-dna build <spec.json> <dna.json> --native-composition <portable-composition.json> [--scope-authority <signed-authority.json>] [--material-root <closed-root>]
```

Inspect compactly, adding independently held trust roots only when available:

```powershell
researchguard domain-dna inspect <dna.json> [--trusted-artifact-sha256 <sha256>] [--trusted-producer-descriptor-fingerprint <sha256>] [--trusted-scope-authority-fingerprint <sha256> | --trusted-scope-authority-producer-descriptor-fingerprint <sha256>] [--material-root <closed-root>] [--member <id> | --behavior <id> | --object <id> | --impact <id> | --reverse <id> | --scope <id>]
```

## Scope before completeness

Every target declares one purpose, `scope_id`, included questions and structure nodes, excluded semantic regions, completion status, and recursive expansion-frontier ids. Every member binds that same scope to its exact complete input and owned-output denominator. The candidate declaration is not its own completeness authority. A separately produced signed scope authority freezes the target/version/material inventory, scope purpose and completion, included structure semantics, required anchors and roles, every member's required input/output kinds, cross-member handoffs, exclusions, frontier, and LogicGuard limits. Its provenance may name a user, target owner, OpenSpec, Spark, ChangeLog, or another external contract, but never the candidate model itself.

The runtime contains no scope-authority private key and trusts no bundled producer by default. A portable signature proves only that the record is internally authentic to its carried public descriptor. `dna_qualified` additionally requires the caller to trust the exact authority fingerprint or producer-descriptor fingerprint out of band. Missing or untrusted authority remains `candidate_model` or `dna_self_consistent`; it never becomes qualified merely because the record travels inside the DNA bundle.

Qualification reports six separate states: `material_inventory_verified`, `member_native_replay_verified`, `candidate_internal_consistency`, `scope_authority_licensed`, `scope_obligation_coverage`, and `dna_qualified`. This makes it possible for another AI to say precisely whether the model is well-formed, natively replayed, externally scoped, complete against that scope, or fully qualified.

A complete closed-directory inventory answers “which bytes were admitted?” It does not answer “were all claims or meanings modeled?” For a bounded question, keep `whole_target_semantics_claimed=false`, name the excluded semantic region, and keep at least one expansion frontier. The canonical Attention Is All You Need example therefore means: all 25 frozen material files are accounted for, and the reported English-to-French BLEU consistency question is deeply modeled. It does not claim to be the complete semantic DNA of the whole paper.

Recursive deepening consumes one frontier by explicitly adding the next question, structure nodes, member inputs, member-owned outputs, bindings, good/bad cases, and evidence. Rebuild the parent mapping and rerun native qualification. Never remove a frontier merely to make the model look complete.

## Binding and failure rules

- Every artifact is a non-embedded path, byte length, and SHA-256 member of one closed directory tree. Omitting or shrinking that inventory fails current-material replay.
- Every source anchor binds one distinct semantic occurrence to one artifact, declared structure node, exact semantic role, and expected structure kind. The current selector is typed: paper examples may use `line_pattern`, while structured models and workflows may use `json_pointer`. Rebinding a table occurrence to prose, or assigning two anchors to one occurrence, fails authority coverage.
- Every member output id has one owner, one model-to-output binding, tests, oracle, and evidence; every parent input/output/handoff is derived exhaustively.
- Removing a binding, structure object, event, claim, scope limit, test, oracle, receipt, manifest case, or opaque payload blocks even if outer JSON identities are recomputed.
- Bundle-only replay can establish candidate self-consistency. `dna_qualified` additionally requires current authority-derived material replay, all trusted artifact identities, trusted native-member producer descriptors, one explicitly trusted signed scope authority, and complete authority-derived obligations. Missing trust or a synchronized candidate shrink stays visible; it never falls back.
- The parent FunctionBlock is checked twice: once for candidate-internal exhaustiveness and once against the authority-derived expected external inputs, member outputs, handoffs, and terminal dispositions. Recomputing all candidate fingerprints cannot shrink that external denominator.
- Authority-required-but-missing objects remain queryable. Impact reports them in the affected authority gap set, and reverse returns `reverse_incomplete` with the authority gap instead of treating the absent candidate object as unknown.
- Within one supplied portable bundle, each `(member_id, receipt_id)` occurs once and its carried reference must equal its signed carried bytes. That is a single-bundle statement. Unless the caller also supplies a shared immutable authority or transparency log, one isolated bundle cannot prove that the producer did not publish a different receipt under the same identity in another isolated bundle.
- The process-local native replay cache is bounded and keyed by the complete bundle bytes plus the normalized exact trust set. It caches no target material result, writes nothing, survives no process, and supplies no stale fallback.

The external DNA can guide another AI through compact member, behavior, object, scope, impact, and reverse queries. The full portable evidence stays in the artifact rather than being injected into the default prompt, preventing token waste while preserving drill-down depth.
